This is the first post of a long road. **The Rewilding Rite** is a pixel-art survival game set in a world that keeps living whether or not you are watching: water feeds plants, plants feed boars, boars feed wolves, and the creatures' brains evolve as they go.

## What the first prototype is

The first public build will be the game **as it is today**, made shareable:

- Linux only, free, on itch.io.
- Unstable. Saves may be wiped between builds. Expect bugs.
- Its job is simple: put the game in your hands and find out what is broken, confusing or boring.

It is not the demo, and it is not the finished game. The first guardian, the Warden, is not part of this build.

## How this devlog works

Posts are honest and regular rather than polished. Each public build gets a post that names what changed because of feedback. If something does not work yet, we will say so.

## What you can do

Follow the devlog by RSS, and when the first build lands on [itch.io](https://arthurmoreno.itch.io/the-rewilding-rite), play it and tell us where you stopped, on the [Discord](https://discord.gg/2mT8XHNwmR).
